Privacy Policy for PassMancer

Effective Date: 19 August 2026
Applicability: Current version of PassMancer
Zero Data Collection No accounts, tracking, ads, or analytics.
100% Offline No INTERNET permission requested.
Device-Only Processing All algorithms run locally on your phone.

PassMancer is an offline password generator and strength analyzer created by Pralhad Nasane.

Your privacy is the core foundation of PassMancer. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and how your privacy is protected.

1. What PassMancer Does

PassMancer helps you:

  • Generate strong, random passwords on your device.
  • Analyze the strength and entropy of passwords.
  • Estimate password cracking times using local mathematical formulas.
  • Temporarily store password history on your device (optional).

All features run entirely on your phone.

2. Information PassMancer Collects

PassMancer collects zero personal data.

  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address.
  • No Personal Information: The app does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
  • No Analytics: The app does not track what you click, how often you use the app, or what features you use.
  • No Crash Reports: The app does not send error logs or crash reports over the internet.
  • No Advertising: The app contains no ads, ad trackers, or advertising SDKs.

3. Internet and Network Access

PassMancer does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not use network connections.

  • All password generation and strength analysis happen locally on your device.
  • The app operates completely offline.

4. How Passwords Are Handled

  • Passwords are generated using Java SecureRandom, a cryptographically secure random number generator (CSPRNG).
  • Password generation and analysis happen locally on your device.
  • PassMancer does not send your passwords or analysis data to a server.

5. Password History (Optional)

Password history is off by default. If you enable it, your generated passwords are stored only in the app's private local storage and are automatically deleted after the retention period you choose (1 to 72 hours).

  • Entry Limits: The history keeps only your chosen maximum number of passwords (default is 10; options: 5, 10, 20, or 50).
  • Manual Deletion: You can delete individual entries or clear your entire history at any time.

6. App Preferences and Settings

PassMancer saves your selected preferences locally on your device:

  • Selected theme (Dark, Light, AMOLED, Midnight)
  • Vibration feedback setting (on/off)
  • Screenshot protection setting (on/off)
  • Password history preferences (enabled state, retention duration, max entries)
  • Onboarding status (whether you have seen the welcome tutorial)

These settings are stored in your device's private app sandbox and are never shared.

7. Clipboard Behavior

PassMancer copies a password to your clipboard only when you tap Copy. On Android 13 and newer, copied passwords are marked as sensitive so Android can hide their preview. PassMancer never reads your clipboard.

8. Screenshot Protection

When Screenshot Protection is enabled, PassMancer prevents screenshots, screen recordings, and password previews from appearing in the recent-apps screen.

9. Backups and Data Transfer

Android cloud and device backups are disabled in PassMancer. Your saved settings and history are not backed up to the cloud or transferred to other devices.

10. Third-Party Libraries

PassMancer uses standard Android open-source components for user interface and local storage. These components run entirely on your device and do not transmit your data.

11. External Links

PassMancer includes external links to rate the app on Google Play and to view the developer's GitHub profile. When you open these external links, the respective third-party service handles your request according to its own privacy policy.

12. How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your locally stored data:

  • Clear Password History: Open the History screen and tap the delete icon, or delete individual items.
  • Clear Cache: Go to Settings > Data Management > Clear Cache.
  • Clear All Data: Go to Settings > Data Management > Clear All Data. This permanently erases all history, resets all settings, and clears cache files.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling PassMancer immediately deletes all locally stored data from your device.

13. Children's Privacy

PassMancer does not collect personal information from its users, including children.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If PassMancer's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. The latest policy is always available inside the app and on the public Privacy Policy page.
